## The Logical Bridge

Tesla confirmed the **effectors** (thrusters, actuators, valves) and confirmed the **autonomy requirement** (humans cannot react fast enough). The only missing piece is the **controller** — and by definition, an autonomous multi-thruster stabilization system *is* a control-system problem.
